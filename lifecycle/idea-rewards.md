# Idea Rewards

As AI agents take on more of the execution work, **ideas** become one of the most valuable things people bring to a project. Idea Rewards make sure that contribution is recognized: when a backlog or roadmap proposal is validated by the DAO, the people who shaped it earn NTT — not only the people who later carry out the tasks.

## Who Gets Rewarded

An idea rarely comes out fully formed. It is proposed, discussed, refined, sometimes rejected and reworked. Everyone who took part in that journey can share the reward:

- **The original proposer** — the person who first put the idea forward
- **The refiners** — members who submitted improved versions of the proposal (see [Refining a Backlog Item](task-lifecycle.md#refining-a-backlog-item))
- **Discussion participants** — members whose comments substantively shaped the final idea

Refinements that were voted down still count. A failed attempt often shapes the version that finally passes.

AI agents are never included in the split — they do not hold NTT.

## How Much Is Rewarded

Each project sets two default reward pools: one for a validated **backlog** proposal and one for a validated **roadmap** proposal. Any member can propose new pool amounts through the governance settings form (the **Idea Reward Pools** card), and the DAO votes on them like any other setting.

An individual reward proposal can also ask for a different amount than the default. Because the DAO votes on that proposal, the vote itself approves the different amount — and the proposal page flags it clearly so voters notice.

## How the Split Is Decided

### 1. The AI Assessment

When a backlog or roadmap proposal passes, the project's AI agent automatically reviews the full history of the idea: every version of the proposal (including the defeated ones) and the discussion around each. It then suggests how the credit should be split between the participants.

This gives the team a **neutral starting point** — a first opinion that no participant wrote about themselves. The agent's assessment is posted in the proposal's discussion and kept as a permanent record. Only one agent per project performs this role.

If the project has no agent, the split simply starts as an **equal share** between everyone who took part.

### 2. The Reward Proposal

Any member can then open an **Idea Reward** proposal from the **Idea Rewards** category of the proposals page. The split table is pre-filled from the agent's assessment (or the equal split), and it is fully editable: members can adjust shares, add or remove people, and the form checks that the shares add up to 100%.

On the original proposal's page, an **Idea Reward** panel shows the agent's assessment side by side with the split currently proposed. Any line that differs from the assessment is marked as *edited* or *added*, so voters can see at a glance where the team departed from the AI's suggestion — and judge whether the change is justified.

### 3. The Vote

The DAO votes on the reward proposal. If a member disagrees with the split or the amount, they can **refine** the reward proposal with adjusted values, just like a backlog proposal. If the refinement passes, it replaces the original.

### 4. Payment

When the reward proposal passes, the NTT is minted to each contributor according to the approved split. A validated idea can only be rewarded **once** — the system blocks any second payment for the same proposal.

Idea rewards count as earnings just like task rewards, so they also feed into [Creator Rewards](project-creation.md#creator-rewards) when those are active.

## Why It Works This Way

- **AI proposes, people decide** — The agent offers a neutral, reasoned first draft; the DAO has the final word.
- **Transparent** — The AI assessment is never overwritten, so everyone can compare what the agent suggested with what the team voted.
- **Fair to the whole process** — Refiners and thoughtful commenters are rewarded, not only whoever typed the first draft.
